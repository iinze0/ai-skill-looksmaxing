# Looksmaxing & Aesthetics Skill

Structured guidance for aesthetic self-improvement and physique goals.

## Core Workflow

1. Gather inputs: current photos description, stats (height, weight, age if given), goals (e.g. Zyzz-style, muscle gain).
2. Break down areas: face (skin, jaw, hair), body (composition, proportions), style, posture.
3. Suggest realistic levers: training, nutrition, skincare, grooming, clothing, lighting for photos.
4. For visualizations: describe ideal before/after prompts or pose recommendations (mirror selfie, specific angles).
5. Prioritize high-impact, sustainable changes over extreme measures.
6. Track progress metrics: weight, measurements, photo consistency.

## Key Principles

- Focus on controllable factors.
- Emphasize consistency and compound progress (training + sleep + diet).
- Photo tips: consistent lighting, angles, timing.

## Common Requests

- Physique projections after X months.
- Pose and lighting advice for Instagram.
- Stack integration with training aesthetics.

Always realistic and non-medical.
